### YouTube scope justification draft

Cryzo connects only the YouTube channel the user authorizes in its Marketing tab. `youtube.readonly` identifies that channel and reads channel/video statistics so the user can confirm the destination and view content performance. `youtube.upload` uploads only the video, title, description and privacy setting that the user reviews and approves. A read-only scope cannot upload; upload alone cannot identify the channel or read its statistics. `yt-analytics.readonly` supplies the authorized channel's analytics report and `openid` identifies the consenting account. Tokens are encrypted on the backend and never returned to the desktop. Cryzo does not request general YouTube account management, scrape unrelated channels, sell YouTube data, or use this data to train models.

## Genuine recording and submission checklist

No demonstration recordings or business documents were supplied during this setup. Do not invent URLs, customer counts, screenshots, business identity, registration documents, review results, or confirmation IDs.

1. Record the production-configured desktop and the app/project identity in the relevant portal. Hide secrets and private unrelated accounts. For Google, include **every OAuth client in the submitted project**.
2. Open Marketing → Channels → Add channel. Show the full real consent screen, requested permissions, approve/deny behavior, callback and connected-account identity.
3. Show account/Page/channel selection where available; verify the destination is visible before composing.
4. Prepare an original, permitted demo asset and post. Show caption/title, media, privacy/audience, preview and explicit confirmation. **Stop before publishing unless the user separately approves that exact post**, even if private or intended only for review. Describe the recording as incomplete if no real upload/publish is demonstrated.
5. Show a successful result and available read-only analytics for the same connected account. Empty or unavailable analytics must be shown honestly.
6. Demonstrate disconnect/reconnect and permission denial as required. Do not show tokens or use another user's content without permission.
7. Submit only a complete truthful application and record the platform confirmation ID and submission date here.
